Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours: long, not hard. No difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no online trophies. One playthrough is enough — but there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so save often.

1 Play the story 60–80 h

Chapters 1–5, plus the side-character questlines as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go, and do optional missions when they show up — any 100 count toward the 100% checklist later.

2 The 100% checklist ~100 h

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The targets are lenient — about 60% true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts.

3 Clean-up ~10 h

Strangers & Freaks strands, the one-off stunts and the miscellaneous trophies, then the platinum pops the moment the last one lands.

Save before every chapter. The game has 15 manual save slots: keep one at the start of each chapter and one untouched from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest.

One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude: meditate with Lucia on the balcony of Jason's house during Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends.

Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats switch off trophies and saving.

About this guide

Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them. Every guide is written for this entry, in our own words, and names its sources beside it — a specific location or step with no source earns no trust, so five trophies whose methods no source had confirmed by 19 Nov 2026 are labelled UNCONFIRMED instead of guessed at. The list was checked against PSNProfiles, Exophase, PowerPyx and Output Lag on 19 Nov 2026; the game released that day, so methods may change as more players finish it.

This is an unofficial fan guide. It uses no Rockstar logo, artwork, box art or game screenshots. It works fully offline: it loads nothing from the network, and your progress never leaves your browser except when you export it yourself.
